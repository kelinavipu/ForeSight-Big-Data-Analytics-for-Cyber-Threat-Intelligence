Cyber Threat Intelligence Academy

Everyday analogies explaining complex cyber attacks and Big Data algorithms in plain English.

📖 Attacks Explained with Everyday Analogies
🛡️ Phishing — "A fake bank letter asking for your PIN" ▼
Plain English: Attackers send fraudulent communications (emails, fake login sites) pretending to come from trusted companies (PayPal, Bank) to trick you into revealing passwords.
MITRE ATT&CK: T1566 - Phishing | Defense: Deploy Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and email domain verification (DMARC).
🛡️ Botnet / C2 — "A remote-controlled zombie army" ▼
Plain English: Malware infects thousands of computers and smart devices, turning them into 'bots' that obey commands from a central hacker server to launch coordinated strikes.
MITRE ATT&CK: T1071 - Application Layer Protocol: C2 | Defense: Block C2 IPs using edge Bloom filters and inspect egress beaconing.
🛡️ DDoS — "Thousands of people blocking a shop entrance" ▼
Plain English: Attackers flood a web server with millions of fake requests until the server runs out of memory and crashes for real customers.
MITRE ATT&CK: T1498 - Network Denial of Service | Defense: Cloud DDoS scrubbing (Cloudflare) and SYN flood cookies.
🛡️ Brute Force — "Trying every key on a massive keyring" ▼
Plain English: Automated scripts guess passwords hundreds of times per second against SSH or login screens until they crack the right password.
MITRE ATT&CK: T1110 - Brute Force | Defense: Account lockout after 5 attempts, Fail2Ban, strong passphrases.
🛡️ Port Scan — "A burglar checking every door and window" ▼
Plain English: Attackers send quick probe packets across all 65,535 network ports to see which services are open and might have vulnerable software.
MITRE ATT&CK: T1046 - Network Service Discovery | Defense: Stealth firewall rules and automated IP blacklisting.
